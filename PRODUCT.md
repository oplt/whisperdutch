# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People watching browser video in Chrome, Brave, Firefox, or another compatible browser who need immediate transcription and translation in a language they understand. They are usually focused on the video and should be able to understand the subtitle window with brief peripheral glances.

## Product Purpose

Provide dependable, low-latency multilingual subtitles and translations from browser audio while keeping audio, transcripts, and model processing local by default. Success means language choice is explicit, controls disappear into the viewing workflow, subtitles remain easy to read, and recovery from backend or capture interruptions is clear and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, dependable. The product should feel like a well-made native utility: calm during normal use, candid about system state, and free of performative decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like collections of cards and status badges, media-player chrome that competes with the video, fake or permanently disabled controls, decorative animation, saturated color, glass effects, gradients, and dense developer telemetry in the primary workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the spoken words visually dominant.
2. Reveal complexity only where it helps the current task.
3. Make system state legible without turning it into a dashboard.
4. Prefer familiar native controls and predictable recovery behavior.
5. Treat privacy and local processing as trustworthy defaults, not promotional decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction behavior. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, browser zoom, narrow windows, and readable subtitle sizing. Do not communicate status through color alone.
